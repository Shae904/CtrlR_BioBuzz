## 2. The robot (CAD facts, mm unless stated)

Frame conventions used everywhere in the sim and in these notes: origin at the robot centre (geometric centre of the
four wheel contact patches) on the floor, **x forward (toward the intake), y left, z up**. Pedro's field frame is
inches, origin at the red/audience corner, x toward blue, y toward the rear wall, heading CCW from +x.

**Drivetrain** `worlds drivetrain:1`
- 4 × goBILDA 104 mm mecanum (3625-xxxx-0104), 4 × Yellow Jacket 435 RPM (5203-2402-0014, 13.7:1, 384.5 PPR).
- Track 387.5 (15.26 in), wheelbase 263.9 (10.39 in). Footprint from centre: +250.8 forward (the intake wheels), −186.9 back, +212.65 left, −242.15 right (the turret's input assembly). 455 × 438 mm, 451 mm tall.
- Motors `fl`/`bl` REVERSE, `fr`/`br` FORWARD (Robot.java). Pedro-tuned: xVelocity 80.21 in/s, yVelocity 65.97 in/s, zero-power decel −57.3 / −64.6 in/s².

**Odometry** (changed 2026-10-21, PR #14)
- goBILDA **Pinpoint** with **two** pods: the **left** parallel pod on the Pinpoint's **X** port, the **strafe** pod on **Y**. The right pod is no longer read. Pods: goBILDA 4-bar, 32 mm wheel, 2000 CPR.
- **Tuned on the robot 2026-10-22** (CommanderSleepy, Pinpoint Tuner): `xPodOffset 7.48184`, `yPodOffset 2.46908`, both pod directions REVERSED. The X offset matches the CAD's 7.5 in. The Y offset came out +2.47 where the CAD puts the strafe pod 2 in *behind* centre (−2.0): either the pod moved or the tuner's spin step read the sign the other way; a quick check is the Pose Test while spinning the robot 360° by hand: sideways drift > 1 in means the Y offset sign is wrong.
- Device name in the robot configuration must be exactly `pinpoint`, on a Control Hub I2C port (1–3; port 0 is the built-in IMU's). As of 10-22 the Pinpoint is confirmed connected and reporting data over ADB.

**Turret** `ghettoturret:1`
- Axis at robot (−13.8, −46.25) mm, i.e. 13.8 behind and 46.25 right of centre; turret plane 247.9 mm above the floor.
- Motor: Yellow Jacket 435 RPM, 2.5:1 spur (40 T) → `Outtake.ticksPerDeg = 2.6701388889` (384.5 × 2.5 / 360). Motor `turret` REVERSE.
- Code convention: turret position = encoder degrees + `AllianceColor.getSteadyState()` (RED 90, BLUE 270). **180 = shooter forward, 90 = shooter pointing left, 270 = right.** So at INIT the shooter must point **left on RED, right on BLUE**; the encoder zeroes there (every opmode). The geometric aim uses this angle + the robot heading for the camera's field heading: a turret off its rest at INIT by 5° costs ~6–14 mm of aim, more if worse.
- Rotating inertia ≈ 0.03 kg·m² (sim estimate). The aim (PR #20, `Subsystems/AimGeometry`): the turret turn that puts the shot line through the CELL opening, from the measured tags (see §4 "Aim"); then (PR #21, `Subsystems/TurretTracker`) the target is held as a **field direction**: a new frame is applied to the turret angle and robot heading **at its capture time** (Limelight capture + targeting latency), only new frames count, and every loop the target follows the robot's heading (+ `AIM_Kv` × turn-rate feed-forward). Tags lost: hold that direction 500 ms (`LOST_HOLD_MS`), then the teleop points at the target cell's opening from odometry (`OPENING_RED_*_IN`, BLUE rotated) until the tags are back, for at most 1.5 s per RB press (`ODOMETRY_PREAIM_TIMEOUT_MS`; then rest, "REST-SEARCH": re-press RB to retry, so a bad odometry heading can't keep the camera pointed away); the autos keep their own pre-aim (`setTarget`). `updateAim` runs every loop (aiming or not), so the delay history is always there and the aim is ready when RB goes down. Limits `TurretTracker.TURRET_MIN/MAX_DEG` 0..360 (seam straight back) with `SEAM_HYSTERESIS_DEG` 15: up to 15° past the seam the turret waits at its limit instead of swinging round. Only tags with a **3D pose** count (the height decides up vs down cell; telemetry warns "Full 3D" if tags come without). Frames asking > 40° (`MAX_FRAME_TURN_DEG`) or results > 300 ms old (`STALE_MS`) are ignored. Known small error: the capture time is taken from the SDK's receipt of the result, which polls every 10 ms, so the delay compensation can be up to 10 ms short. Flywheel default before any tag reading: `DEFAULT_SHOT_TICKS` 1150. PID `Limelight.AIM_Kp/Kd/Ks` are still DECODE's: tune with Panels' Graph of "Turret error" (teleop telemetry). Closed-loop model (approximate turret: ~1040°/s, 60 ms response): old logic overshot 14.5°, hunted ±5.2° standing still, lagged 13° with the robot turning 90°/s; new: 1.1°, ±0.1°, 2.5°.

**Shooter** (flywheel + hood)
- 2 × Yellow Jacket 6000 RPM (5203-2402-0001) on a 1:1 belt, 28 CPR → "ticks/s" in telemetry is encoder counts/s (1140 ticks/s ≈ 2440 RPM). Full power ≈ 2300 ticks/s (`kV = 0.0004348`). `flywheel1` FORWARD, `flywheel2` REVERSE; velocity is read from `flywheel1`. Rotor inertia 6.06e-4 kg·m² (Fusion mass properties).

The ball drops into the gap between the two tires and rides each tire's inner shoulder (not the 96 mm OD). Hood exit tangent 48°; launch point 4.8 mm ahead of the turret axis, **378 mm above the floor**.
- **Launch angle: ~52.6° since the 2026-11 hood change (team measurement, 2026-11-11).** History: Logger Pro measured ~58° on 2026-10-21 with the previous hood; ghettoturret v11 (saved 2026-10-23) has `HoodExitAngle` 53° (v10: 48°). The CAD has not been re-checked against the new hood (Fusion was not running on 11-11).
- Limelight mount checked in the CAD on 2026-11-04 (v11): the visible mount `LIMELIGHT3ACAD_STEP (2):3` has the lens at (−81.5, 64.4, 193.3) mm in the turret frame (81.5 right of the shot line, 64.4 behind the axis, 35.0° up, yaw 0.00°) = `AimGeometry`/`Limelight`. The hidden old mount `:2` is on the **left**, level, 61.7 behind: if the robot ever carries that one, the aim geometry must change.

**Limelight 3A** (on the turret, `LIMELIGHT3ACAD_STEP (2):3`)

## 4. The shot formula (history matters here)

`Limelight.calculateTargetVelocity()`: `ticks = (d·(SHOT_A·d − SHOT_B)^−0.5 / SHOT_K + SHOT_OFFSET) / SHOT_ETA − vParallel·SHOT_MOVE_GAIN + SHOT_TRIM_TICKS`, with `d` = camera→opening horizontal distance in m (from `AimGeometry`), rounded to 20. `TOF_A/B` give time of flight for leading moving shots.

**Current fit (PR #30, 2026-11-11): 52.6° launch, efficiency as its own number.** `SHOT_A 0.280894269484, SHOT_B 0.240816326531, SHOT_K 0.0024778778818, SHOT_OFFSET −264` are the speed a 52.6° launch needs if the ball left at the full rolling speed (5.265 mm/s per tick/s; 2.80 in pollen, launch 378 mm, opening 1.509 m, drag + backspin lift; fit error < 1 tick/s over 1.4–3.6 m; `TOF_A 1.505, TOF_B 1.44`). **`SHOT_ETA` = 0.947** is the fraction of that speed the ball really gets; it scales the whole curve, so it is the one number to calibrate: shots long → raise it, short → lower it; from one spot, new = old × (formula's speed) / (speed that lands). Gives 1.4 m 1250, 1.54 m 1220, 2.0 m 1225, 2.5 m 1290, 3.0 m 1370 (camera→opening). **Where 0.947 comes from:** the team said to ignore the old 1140-from-the-corner number because it was taken at the old hood angle, so it is no longer a target; but that test taken at the angle the old hood really launched at (~58°, Logger Pro) measures this shooter's efficiency: 1140 ticks/s scoring from 1.54 m at 58° ⇒ 0.947 (0.933 at the best possible angle; the earlier "eta 1.013" was the same test wrongly assumed to be 52.6°). **Not yet confirmed with the new hood**; a no-loss shooter (1.00) would be 1155 at 1.54 m. The 58° variant and its Panels switch are gone (the hood is no longer 58°). Field tools: telemetry "Trims" shows `SHOT_ETA`; `SHOT_TRIM_TICKS` (gp2 dpad up/down ±10 in auto-speed mode), "TOO CLOSE" under `TOO_CLOSE_M` 1.3 m, and a distance cross-check (3D distance vs the distance from the tags' known height and the camera pitch; off by > 15% → check the pipeline's marker size 82.55 mm).

History: PR #13 (2026-10-19) fitted `SHOT_K 0.0025098411518, SHOT_OFFSET −261` (same A/B) = this curve at eta 1.013, i.e. anchored on 1140 from the auto corner assuming 52.6°; PR #24/#26 added and then switched off a 58° variant (`SHOT58_*`, also anchored on 1140).

Before that: the 10-16 constants (eta 0.859, the DECODE robot's 14% slip, soft foam ball) asked for 1429 at the corner; 1140 scoring there means at least 0.933 at any launch angle, so this shooter loses much less than DECODE's.

Manual presets `Outtake.medSpeed 1380 / farSpeed 1560` are still the 10-16 model's values (left alone at Ryan's request, 11-04); the current formula gives ~1225 at 2.0 m and ~1400 at 3.2 m.

Flywheel control: `power = (kS + kV·target + kP·error) · 12 / Vbatt` (`Outtake`, voltage-compensated). **Soft start (PR #27, faster + teleop/test only since PR #28):** a jump from rest asked for full power at zero speed (full torque) and the belts skipped; when the target rises while the flywheel is below `SOFT_START_BELOW_TICKS` 700, the controller's setpoint ramps linearly from the flywheel's speed at `SOFT_START_RATE` 600 ticks/s per s (never more than `SOFT_START_MAX_LEAD` 200 ahead): 0→1000 ≈ 1.9 s, 0→1150 ≈ 2.1 s, 0→1560 ≈ 2.8 s, peak torque ~37% of stall instead of 100% (model). **Never in `@Autonomous` opmodes** (checked on the opmode's class in `Outtake`'s constructor): the autos spin up exactly as before. A running flywheel's target changes, shot recovery and slowing down are unchanged (identical in the model); `atTarget`/`getTargetVelocity` use the real target, "Vel Setpoint" in telemetry shows the ramp. The FLYWHEEL PID TESTING sweep ramps its open-loop power (`FF_POWER_RAMP` 0.3/s), its step test's first step from rest shows the ramp.

**Shot-recovery experiments (PR #32, 2026-11-11; NOT permanent):** the first ball of a burst lands, the next ones fall short (the flywheel slows with each ball despite the steel flyweights). `Outtake.RECOVERY_MODE` (a Panels dropdown, one at a time, default `OFF` = the plain controller, identical to before) picks an experiment; teleop + test opmodes only (never `@Autonomous`), and only on a flywheel above 700 ticks/s that is not soft-starting. `STIFFER_GAIN` (kP × `RECOVERY_KP_MULT` 4 below target), `FEED_POWER` (+`RECOVERY_FEED_POWER` 0.12 while the gate is open), `OVERSPEED_AFTER_FIRST_BALL` (after the first ball is seen, aim +`RECOVERY_OVERSPEED_TICKS` 40 until the gate closes), `KICK_ON_EACH_BALL` (full power `RECOVERY_KICK_MS` 80 after each ball seen), `FULL_POWER_BELOW_TARGET` (bang-bang beyond `RECOVERY_BAND_TICKS` 20), `PACED_FEED` (the intake waits while more than `RECOVERY_READY_TICKS` 20 below target; `Outtake.feedAllowed()`). Balls are "seen" as a fall of `RECOVERY_SHOT_DROP_TICKS` 30 from the recent speed with the gate open (`setFeeding`; hub speed comes in steps of 20, so small drops are marginal). Telemetry "Recovery": mode, balls seen, worst drop; FLYWHEEL PID TESTING circle + right trigger reports each shot's drop and recovery time. Model (real `Outtake.java` on a simulated flywheel, 4% per ball, a ball every 180 ms; ball speeds as % of the first): OFF 98.0 / 97.0 / 96.6; STIFFER 100; FEED_POWER 101.3 first then 100.0–99.2; OVERSPEED 99.5–99.7; KICK 100.3–100.7; FULL_POWER 98.9–99.2; PACED 98.8–99.1 at twice the burst time. When one is chosen: make it permanent (and decide about the autos), delete the rest. `kS 0, kV 0.0004348, kP 0.003`; the team tuned kS/kV/kP in Panels on 10-19 ("to our liking"), **check that the values in `Outtake.java` are the tuned ones**, Panels edits don't persist. `FLYWHEEL PID TESTING` opmode: triangle = kS/kV sweep, square = step test, circle = hold + shot recovery.

Aim (PR #20, `Subsystems/AimGeometry`, fed by `Limelight.updateHiveTarget`): each visible tag of the target cell gives its horizontal bearing (tx/ty **without the Limelight crosshair**, through the 35° pitch) and range (3D pose, else the up-cell height); from its known place in the row (ids firstID..+3 = −6.5, −2.75, +2.75, +6.5 in, left→right as seen from the side the cell faces; "right" = field +x for audience cells, −x for scoring cells, checked on the field CAD) each gives the cluster centre, so a missing tag no longer moves the aim. The opening is 73 mm from the centre toward the shooter; with the camera 64.4 mm behind the turret axis and 81.5 mm right of the shot line that gives the exact turn (`aimCorrectionDeg`) and the formula distance (launch→opening + 69 mm). Row direction / opening step come from the camera's field heading (robot heading + turret angle): 5° wrong ≈ 6–14 mm. Standalone test (field CAD tags, 4 cells × 9 spots × 8 headings × turret off ±20°, every tag subset): 0.00 mm with exact data. One trim for anything left over (camera yaw, sideways throw): `Limelight.AIM_TRIM_DEG` (+ = right), same for every spot and both alliances. **Per-cell trims** (PR #31 blue scoring, PR #33 all four; Panels, default 0, + = right): `Limelight.AIM_TRIM_RED_SCORING_DEG`, `AIM_TRIM_RED_AUDIENCE_DEG`, `AIM_TRIM_BLUE_AUDIENCE_DEG`, `AIM_TRIM_BLUE_SCORING_DEG` are added on top only while the turret aims at that cell, teleop and autos (the team saw shots at blue scoring go to the outside). Teleop telemetry "Cell aim trim" shows the targeted cell's value and field name. If a miss turns out to depend on where the robot shoots from, it is geometry (heading / that pipeline's settings), not an offset. **Replaced** the DECODE constants `AllianceColor.getAimPose()` (RED −5, BLUE −1, never refit; they missed by up to ~12 cm, BLUE ~11 cm right from its own corner) and the autos' per-spot `AIM_TX_*` trims. Teleop telemetry "Aim": tags used, turn, opening distance.

- **None of this has run on the real robot yet.** Things to expect to tune: aim points, feed timing (`FEED_FIRST_MS 150`, `FEED_INTERVAL_MS 233`, `FEED_MARGIN_MS 110`), whether the real intake grabs pollen pressed against the wall and out of the flower, Pedro's following accuracy (then the settle thresholds).
- Shooting geometry limits (from the sim): from closer than ~1.2 m the ball can't climb to the opening (needs ≥0.96 m horizontal at any speed); comfortable 1.3–1.45 m; the speed formula has a singularity below ~1.3 m (`SHOT_A·d − SHOT_B` → 0). From the spilled-pollen pile right under the hive you cannot score: back off to ~1.4 m first.

## 7. Teleop (`TeleOp/BaseTeleop`, RED/BLUE)

Field-centric drive on gamepad1 left stick, turn right stick (either pad). Left bumper (gp1) resets yaw. **RB (either pad) = auto-aim + auto flywheel speed** from the Limelight. Triangle = flapper open (SHOOT). The **light** (`rgb`) shows aimed (0.5) when the turret error is under half the opening's window from where the robot is (`AIM_WINDOW_FRACTION`, capped at `AIM_RGB_THRESHOLD` 2°; it was a fixed 4°); telemetry "Aim" shows the source (VISION / HOLD / ODOMETRY), tags, turn, distance and "AIMED". gp1 square no longer does anything (the DECODE "reset" spun the turret at full power while held: removed in PR #21). **gamepad2 circle ○ (Xbox B) toggles our AUDIENCE ↔ SCORING cell** (gamepad2 only, PR #29; the PS names like the other buttons, the SDK sets both names from the same button) (both alliances; short rumble; no automatic switching since PR #24; gamepad1 no longer switches cells). **gp2 back (View) = odometry reset at the audience wall** (longer rumble). **gp2 dpad in auto-speed mode = field trims**: up/down flywheel ±10 ticks/s (`SHOT_TRIM_TICKS`), right/left aim ±0.5° (`AIM_TRIM_DEG`, + = right); they last until the app restarts or code is deployed, telemetry "Trims" shows them: copy them into `Limelight.java`. **gp1 dpad up stops the flywheel** (was dpad down, PR #29); **gp1 dpad down toggles the flower** down/up, gp2 cross holds it down; gp2 right/left stick buttons toggle auto speed; with auto off, gp2 dpad up/down/right = far/med/cycle presets, gp2 LB = one formula read. Triggers = intake in/out. (The gp1 dpad up/left tilt bindings were removed by CommanderSleepy on 2026-11-10.) Telemetry goes to both the DS and Panels (`Robot` wraps `opMode.telemetry` in Panels' `JoinedTelemetry`, PR #12) so Panels' Graph can plot `Vel Current`, `Vel Target` etc. The Graph plots nothing until you pick series ("All"), one shared y-axis, 75 ms updates.

**Limelight cell targeting** (manual since PR #24; PR #18 had odometry zones): the drivers toggle the cell with **gp2 circle/B** (AUDIENCE: RED 34–37 pipeline 5, BLUE 38–41 pipeline 6 ↔ SCORING: RED 30–33 pipeline 3, BLUE 42–45 pipeline 4); gamepad1 has no cell button; at INIT the teleop starts on the cell of the field half the robot is in (`HiveTargeting.initialCell`, after an auto: the side it parked on). A cell's tags face its own side, so you only see it from there; both buttons work in INIT too. `Limelight.targetCell` switches the pipeline once per change (retried every 250 ms if it fails) and also ignores the other cell's tag IDs. The target cell can be the one that is DOWN: then there is no aim (correct: you can't score into it), drive to the other side. **Start pose:** Pedro starts every opmode at (0, 0), so the teleop seeds it: the AUTO's last pose if an auto ran ≤ 180 s ago (`PoseStorage.MAX_AGE_S`), used once; otherwise it assumes the robot is at the alliance's AUTO start spot. Telemetry shows which ("Pose from"), plus target cell, pipeline, pose. The pose is now only used for the turret's pre-aim when no tags are in view. **Odometry reset (PR #19, gp2 back since PR #24, INIT or match):** push the robot against the **audience wall** (the wall facing the crowd, the same wall for both alliances), anywhere along it, any face: y = the centre's distance from that wall for the current heading (`HiveTargeting.audienceWallY`, footprint `FRONT/BACK/LEFT/RIGHT_IN` 9.87/7.36/8.37/9.53 in: 7.36 backed on at 90°, up to 13.72 with the front-right corner on it); x and heading are kept. Only the audience wall: pressing it anywhere else sets y wrong (press it again at the audience wall).

## 8. Pitfalls we've hit (so you don't)

- Panels `@Configurable` edits don't survive a restart; copy tuned values into the code.
- SDK merges overwrite `build.dependencies.gradle`: re-add the bylazar + dairy repos, revhub, tuning and fullpanels after any SDK update. Panels must be ≥1.0.15 on SDK 12. Sloth 0.3.2 comes from the Dairy repo and is required (Panels configurables/opmodecontrol and Pedro tuning need it).
- `tuning:1.0.2` crashes Sloth at robot start (TunerScanner NPE); `1.0.3-beta.1` is what we run (the 10-22 "disconnected" scare turned out to be the Pinpoint missing from the robot configuration, not the library; the tuners worked once it was configured).
- Pedro 3 `ConfigVar<Double>` must be set with a double literal (`-2.0`, not `-2`).
- The Limelight web UI "ignores NetworkTables index" while you have a pipeline selected there: click the button or the code can't switch pipelines.
- Logger Pro angle: the camera must be perpendicular to the shot plane or the angle reads steep.
- The Pinpoint's IMU calibrates for ~0.25–0.5 s after INIT/reset: keep the robot still.
- Pedro's pose starts at (0, 0) in every opmode: anything that needs the field position in the teleop needs `PoseStorage` (or a known start spot).
- The turret encoder zeroes at every INIT wherever the turret is: it must point **left (RED) / right (BLUE)** then (teleop INIT telemetry reminds). Off by X there, the rest direction, the back seam and the aim's camera heading are all off by X.
- HIVE 4 (comp 1 values): at the shooting spot (12, 20) the robot's back-right corner reaches the red wall (x ≈ −0.04 in) at its −52.8° shooting heading, and the straight park line (12, 20) → (14, 106) passed the red-wall flower (plate x 0–4.9, y 44.2–50.1) with 0.05 in to spare: now a Bézier through (34, 64) (PR #23). The shooting spot itself is unchanged.
- `Outtake.medSpeed/farSpeed` (1380 / 1560, manual presets with auto speed off) are the old model's; the current formula gives ~1146 at 2 m, ~1311 at 3.2 m.
- An annotation check like `getClass().isAnnotationPresent(Autonomous.class)` inside a subsystem tests the subsystem's own class, never the opmode's: it is always false.
- Both hives' tags are only visible from the side the up cell faces (down + outward); from behind a hive you see nothing. The autos' shooting headings are chosen so the cell is in the turret's rest direction on arrival.
